# Ugliness Presets

Choose one dominant preset. Mix at most two secondary cues so the result remains intentionally directed rather than arbitrarily unfinished.

## Preset Matrix

| Preset | Dominant cues | Best fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rainbow-chaos` | Saturated collisions, rainbow borders, mismatched section fills | General-purpose default |
| `geocities` | Tiled patterns, counters, badges, marquees, early-web typography | Personal pages and nostalgic experiments |
| `discount-mall` | Red/yellow urgency, price bursts, dense offers, bevel buttons | Stores, campaigns, and promotional pages |
| `office-art` | WordArt-like headings, clip-art, gradients, heavy shadows | Presentations, internal tools, novelty portfolios |
| `portal-2005` | Dense navigation, blue panels, tables, link directories | Portals, dashboards, documentation indexes |
| `visual-accident` | All conflict dimensions with incompatible historical cues | Art pieces and maximum-intensity requests |

## Rainbow Chaos

- Use six or more saturated colors and multiple border colors.
- Change font, fill, and radius families between adjacent sections.
- Prefer confetti, checkerboard, zigzags, and hard color bands.
- Keep one visible image or media asset as an anchor for the actual subject.

## Geocities

- Use repeating background tiles and visible section boundaries.
- Use monospace, serif, and novelty display typography together.
- Add decorative status badges, separators, counters, or marquee-like motion.
- Keep all nostalgic elements original or clearly reusable; do not scrape private legacy assets.

## Discount Mall

- Use saturated red, yellow, cyan, and lime with thick outlines.
- Use starbursts, crossed-out prices, coupon shapes, and dense product grids.
- Preserve truthful prices and labels. Never fabricate stock counts, testimonials, security badges, or deadlines.

## Office Art

- Use oversized outlined headings, mismatched gradients, and long shadows.
- Combine clip-art-like visuals with spreadsheet or slide-deck visual language.
- Use bevels and inconsistent alignment while keeping data relationships understandable.

## Portal 2005

- Use dense link groups, table-like modules, narrow columns, and small utility text.
- Mix blue gradients with unrelated alert colors and decorative separators.
- Maintain touch targets and responsive access even when the desktop presentation is dense.

## Visual Accident

- Require all eight conflict dimensions.
- Pull cues from at least three other presets with no harmonious transition.
- Use only with `ugliness: 5` unless the user explicitly requests a milder result.
- Keep the safety floor intact even when using `full-chaos` mode.

## Asset Guidance

- Use user-provided, generated, original, public-domain, or clearly licensed assets.
- Prefer bitmap textures, product imagery, portraits, clip-art-like objects, and stickers that reveal the actual subject.
- Use bundled patterns and stickers as secondary decoration, not as a substitute for subject-specific media.
- Avoid dark, blurred, or atmospheric hero imagery when the actual product or object should be inspectable.
